9 — LED-Only Color Policy

VIOLATION — rgba(79, 209, 197, 0.6) in RadialButton hover glow.
LED signal colors (--led-ok, --led-warn, --led-fault) must NEVER appear in 2D chrome outside .led elements. No buttons, badges, loaders, or pills.

ALLOWED — LED signal colors on .led[data-state] elements only. The same teal (#14b8a6) is used for Atlas wire cores — intentional, semantic: healthy connection.
